Privacy Policy for Zakaz-AF

Last updated: February 02, 2026

At Zakaz-AF, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, place an order, or contact customer support. This includes:

  • Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Delivery address and location data for order fulfillment.
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party providers).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Provide customer support and send service notifications.
  • Improve our application and user experience.
  • Ensure the security and safety of our platform and users.

3. Sharing of Information

We only share your information with:

  • Local Shops: Your name and address so they can fulfill your order.
  • Delivery Partners: Your contact and address info for delivery.
  • Service Providers: For services like hosting, payment processing, and analytics.

4. Your Rights

You can access, update, or delete your personal information at any time within the application settings or by contacting our support team.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.
